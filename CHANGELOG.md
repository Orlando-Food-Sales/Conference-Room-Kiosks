# Changelog

## 2.2.0 — 2026-11-17
- Birthdays and anniversaries list: it now reads the whole calendar (500 events at a time, up to 20,000). It used to stop after 1,000 events, and the shared calendar has over 2,100 (every PTO day and holiday too), so about half of the birthdays were missing from the list. If it ever hits the limit it says the list may be missing people.
- Dashboard: **Birthdays and work anniversaries** panel. It lists everyone from the shared calendar (sorted by next date, with search and a filter), can **Hide** a person from the door screens without touching the calendar (saved in `data/celebration-hidden.json`), **Add** a person (yearly all-day event in the format the displays read) and **Remove from calendar** the whole yearly series of a work anniversary in the app's own format after a confirmation (it lands in Outlook's Deleted Items; birthdays other people entered are hide-only). New endpoints `GET /api/admin/celebrations` and `POST /api/admin/celebrations/hide|add|remove`, behind the usual admin token or sign-in. Every change is logged as `Celebrations: ...` and shown under Recent activity. Works in demo mode. Needs `Calendars.ReadWrite` and an editable calendar for add and remove. New tests in `test/celebration-admin.test.js`; `tools/e2e-celebrations.mjs` drives the panel in headless Chrome.
- Dashboard additions: a **System** box (server version and uptime, whether Microsoft sign-in is on, and days left on the Microsoft 365 app secret, the HTTPS certificate and the certificate renewal login, red when a reminder is due); a **Recent activity** list (last 30 door camera uses and switches, intercom calls with who answered, sign-ins and removed tablets, kept in memory, never any sound or pictures); tablets show their model (D138) instead of "Android"; and a **Remove** button on offline tablets to clear old entries (for example after a tablet moved from http to https). `POST /api/admin/tablets/forget` refuses a tablet that is online. 3 new tests (268 total).
- The red "Microphone on" popup on the door tablet is removed as well, on request. A call still needs the person at the door to press **Call** and someone to answer, and the call panel shows on the tablet during the call. The noise meter measures with nothing shown on the tablet and only sends a level number.
- The red "Camera on" popup on the door tablet while an admin uses the door camera is removed, on request. Every use is still logged with who looked. (The "Microphone on" badge for the intercom and noise meter stays.)
- Door intercom: a **Call** button on the kiosk tablets (`?kiosk=1`) rings every signed-in dashboard. The person at the door always starts the call; nothing on the dashboard can start one. The first administrator to press **Answer** takes it (a second one is told "Already answered by ..."). The tablet's microphone is not opened until someone has answered, and a red **Microphone on** badge shows while it is. Sound goes straight between the tablet and the administrator's browser (WebRTC, no STUN or TURN, so office network or VPN only) and never passes through, or is stored by, the server. The log has who answered and how long it lasted, never any sound or connection data. A call ends from either side, after `INTERCOM_MAX_MINUTES`, or when the administrator closes the dashboard.
- Noise level: each tablet measures how loud its room is (quiet, talking or loud, with the dB number) and the dashboard shows it. Only a number leaves the tablet, and the red "Microphone on (noise level only)" badge is visible on the tablet while it runs, the same badge as in a call. It stops when the page is hidden, the display is off or a call is running. The thresholds are first guesses: read the dB numbers for a few days and set `NOISE_TALKING_DB` and `NOISE_LOUD_DB`.
- Tablet location: each tablet reports its position (read at start and every 6 hours) and the dashboard shows it with an **Open map** link (OpenStreetMap, opened only when clicked), or says why it cannot (permission, plain http, no fix).
- These three need the tablets on the https address (a browser only allows microphone and location on https). The tablets report `secure`, and the dashboard warns about a tablet that is still on plain http. `Permissions-Policy` now allows microphone and geolocation for the page itself. New settings: `INTERCOM_ENABLED`, `INTERCOM_RING_SECONDS`, `INTERCOM_MAX_MINUTES`, `NOISE_METER`, `NOISE_TALKING_DB`, `NOISE_LOUD_DB`, `LOCATION_REPORT`. Setup: `INSTALL-UBUNTU.md` 12f.
- `tools/e2e-intercom.mjs` drives a real headless Chrome through the whole thing (`node tools/e2e-intercom.mjs`). The project version is now 2.2.0.
